# Two-human-player pass

The combined aid is intentionally solo-only. The 2P pass is therefore a mode-aware replacement layer over a completed solo app, sourced from Rules and the Crown Handbook. It is not a small appendix limited to Rules pp43–46.

## Solo shortcuts that require replacement in 2P

### Actor language and ordinary negotiation

Generic “you” becomes the acting/affected human. Restore ordinary human-to-human transfers, promise cards, binding immediate deals, non-binding future deals, no double activation, and non-transferable assets from Rules p8. Normal difficulty forbids exchanging human promise cards for Crown cubes; it does not erase ordinary promises between humans. All negotiations stop at Game End.

### Family and London order

Family actions proceed from Chairman clockwise. Prestige-card order is highest retirement spend, then Windows, then clockwise from Prime Minister. These are three-family order rules, not Crown-versus-one-human choices.

### Chairman election

Replace aid p4's two-family shortcut with Rules p15:

- declare the pure-majority threshold (> half all Company shares);
- any family with a share can campaign;
- support from several families can combine;
- first candidate reaching threshold wins;
- if none succeeds, former Chairman chooses any eligible shareholder, including self;
- an empty Court returns former Chairman with a supply piece, then a prize piece if necessary, otherwise remains vacant.

Apply the Crown support/elect-Crown favors only where their exact predicates hold.

### Nepotism and general hiring

Every other family in the candidate pool must consent to a hirer selecting their own family. A human may therefore need consent from both the other human and Crown. Keep Crown's candidate priority and AI ties; add the explicit 2P unanimous branch above.

### Debt consent

Every advance beyond the first three needs support from a majority of shares. The two humans can jointly provide that majority. Crown-consent favor prices appear only when Crown support is used.

### Choices among human assets

Any Crown rule that says “your” ship, Writer, Officer, office, or piece must consider both humans separately. Preserve explicit type/region priorities first; use Button only if the remaining choice is between humans. This affects Director transfers, Shipping, Military Affairs, Commander exhaustion, Board of Control, War Against France, and similar law effects.

### Armies, Commanders, and loot

Compare each family's pieces independently for Commander eligibility. When dividing loot, pay Commander/own Officers, then other families clockwise, then Regiments/alliances as the base rule directs. Button is not a generic substitute for clockwise order.

### Parliament

Replace both solo two-family branches with Rules pp35–36:

- PM begins voting; proceed clockwise in repeated rounds;
- either human may support or oppose, subject to PM honor;
- opposition totals and coalitions are per family;
- the highest opposition total holds Opposition Leader; incumbent wins a tie;
- on ordinary law failure, actual Opposition Leader becomes PM;
- Crown follows its special timing/behavior, including voting last and against only for Deregulation;
- Crown policy-space comparison counts affected human entities collectively, while laws using “most” compare Crown against each family separately.

The aid sentence that automatically makes Crown Opposition Leader/new PM under a human PM is solo-only behavior and must disappear in 2P.

### Firms

Restore base multiplayer procedures omitted by “never occurs in solo”:

- human investment needs manager consent and continues until no human wishes to act;
- firm disputes use manager/PM rules, not Button by default;
- hostile takeover requires no currently managed firm and majority shareholder consent;
- mergers require every shareholder's consent and resolve primary firm, transferred shares, treasury, ships, and 10-share limit;
- ship acquisition still requires the other human's consent where applicable;
- strategy and trade spend are secret/simultaneous at the physical table;
- multiple firms and Company trade by initiative; President/Chairman resolves ties as written;
- firm revenue uses initiative/order; a failing firm may receive human emergency investment or emergency merger;
- a shareholder can waive dividends only with manager consent;
- Crown dividend reward compares Crown's share dividend with **each** other family and excludes manager's extra payment.

Do not build secret choices on the shared phone screen in a way that reveals them. Give procedure and let players use physical cards/cubes.

### Final scoring

- Award first and second Power prizes.
- If tied for most, each tied player receives the lower award and no other award is made.
- A tie for second receives nothing.
- Remove solo Company-failure VP adjustment.
- Break final VP tie by Windows, then clockwise from Prime Minister—not simply “Prime Minister wins.”

## Player Button transition policy

Automate a pass only when the supplied source explicitly does so:

1. Crown chooses between humans;
2. humans dispute who may act;
3. Crown firm investments, where H3 explicitly says switch after each.

For H13 writer priority, pass when the actual selection is between eligible human owners under R44. Do not pass when placing a Crown Writer or when only one human has an eligible Writer. For the generic free-choice clarification and H14 Crisis chooser, show the current holder and a manual pass control until the source ambiguity is ruled on.

## Acceptance cases for the later 2P pass

1. 4/4/4 cube setup and 4/4/4 setup cards; Hard and Expert adjustments preserve a 12-cube pool.
2. Crown takes one normal Family action plus any free/extra actions.
3. Humans jointly elect a Chairman although no single family holds > half.
4. Human A hires self while human B and Crown both have candidates; both consents are handled.
5. Crown hiring with both humans eligible and only one consenting pays no false reward.
6. Both humans have eligible ships; Crown selection follows Button and passes. One eligible human causes no pass.
7. Human Writers are allocated after Crown Writers and Button changes only on actual human arbitration.
8. Human B becomes Opposition Leader/new PM under human A's failed law.
9. Two firms trade in one Presidency without exposing secret strategies prematurely.
10. Crown dividend reward compares against each family, not aggregate or manager alone.
11. Two favors compete for the Crown's last cube; Button resolves right to act and passes.
12. First-place Power tie, second-place tie, final VP tie, and Company failure all use 2P scoring.

## Open source edges

The implementation can remain source-exact without automating these until resolved:

- Does Button pass after a generic free choice or Crisis-defense choice when the choice is not explicitly between humans?
- What, if any, promise reward applies when Crown nepotism needs consent and only one human is in the candidate pool?
- Rules p38 requires manager consent for investment, while the Handbook makes the paid denial favor solo-only. Can a 2P manager freely refuse Crown investment, or does Crown behavior override ordinary manager consent?
- The books do not define Legendary difficulty for 2P.
